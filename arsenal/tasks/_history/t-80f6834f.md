**Severity:** medium · **Confidence:** medium · **Category:** performance

**Where:** `src/render/document.ts:47-65, src/render/document.ts:16, src-tauri/src/document.rs:8`

### Finding
parseLarge gives a worker 10 s; on timeout it terminates it and runs renderChunks(source) synchronously on the UI thread. A document slow enough to time out (up to the 20 MB cap, or pathological nesting) then freezes the window for at least as long, with decorations drawn by the page so the user cannot even close it.

### Evidence
Traced the control flow: dropWorker(undefined) resolves pending with null -> falls through to `return renderChunks(source)`. Timing on a 20 MB input not measured.

### Suggested direction
On timeout show an error/'render as plain text' fallback rather than re-parsing on the main thread; distinguish 'worker failed to start' from 'worker too slow'.
